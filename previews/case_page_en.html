The challenge

02 / PROBLEM

One good image is easy. The same woman in the fortieth is the job.

Everything that existed of Zayra lived in the dark: wet streets, smoke, a single spotlight. The campaign had to take her into daylight — new locations, new wardrobe, five lighting conditions, stills and moving image — without losing a single trait of who she is.

  • Same face from close-up to wide, front to profile to back.
  • Same character from cold dawn to tungsten night.
  • One production — not a pile of generations.

Creative direction

03 / DECISION

One day, five lights. The song says it — “with my lights and my scars” — so the campaign follows the light, from the cold blue before sunrise to the bulbs of the night. The leather jacket is the thread through every chapter.
